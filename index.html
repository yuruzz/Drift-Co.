Brand Narrative

"Why Drift & Co.?"

DRIFT & CO. started its humble beginning in 2010. We use only world-class and premium fragrance oils imported directly from the United States and Germany.

We put 30% fragrance oil (one of the highest concentrations in the market) into our perfumes, which makes the scents extraordinarily long-lasting.

We offer inspiration scents from 100 popular world-class brands so our consumers enjoy an expansive variety of scents to choose from. This is fundamentally different from cheap "imitations" and generic "Class A" perfume products.

We mix our own perfumes and champion our own brand. Our bottles are sleek, classy, and perfect for gifts—all while remaining very affordable for everyday use.
